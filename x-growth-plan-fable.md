Honest odds of crossing 5M by Oct 15 with full execution: **~40–50%.** Odds that

## 5. The cornerstone: launching stratus

### 5.1 The strategic case (why your instinct is right)

- **The audience is the user base.** Build-in-public X is the one place where an X
  growth tool is *meta-viral*: every person who sees the launch is a potential user,
  and every user tweets with it, about it.
- **It fixes your biggest content weakness.** Your median post is a general opinion
  (127–295 views) because there's no artifact behind it. After the launch, every daily
  post has a spine: a feature, a number, a cost dashboard, a war story. Milestone and
  data_comparison — your two highest-median formats — currently starve for material;
  a launch produces it weekly (star counts, install counts, issue stories, the 5M race
  itself).
- **It creates 3 scheduled viral-class moments** instead of waiting for lottery luck.
- **The meta-loop is the moat:** *"51-year-old builder wrote his own X growth OS and is
  racing to monetization by Oct 15, all numbers public, tool open source, costs him
  $0.15/day."* Nobody on the timeline has this story. If you get monetized using it,
  the tool's marketing is complete; if you don't, the honest post-mortem is itself a
  banger. Heads you win, tails you win.

### 5.2 Positioning: the anti-SaaS

**Free. Open source (MIT). Bring your own keys. Self-hosted. $0.15/day to run.**
"The anti-SaaS X growth stack" — this is your niche's exact value system
(cost-conscious, tool-first, no hype) turned into a product identity. Do **not** launch
it as a paid SaaS now: monetizing stratus before Oct 15 is a second full-time job that
competes with the impressions goal, and a price tag kills the story's purity. A hosted
version is a *post-October* decision, made from a position of 10k followers and a
starred repo.

### 5.3 The entry-barrier problem, solved in tiers

Be honest in the README about the funnel, and give three tiers:

- **Tier 0 — $0, five minutes, no server, no X API:** the extension alone with a BYO
  LLM key. The $0 DOM-scrape surfaces already do the magic without the API: Radar
  (reply queue + banding), Harvest, Voice/swipe file, the static Coach, canned reply
  lists. This is the "very low entry barrier" you asked for, and it's ~90% built —
  it needs a `liteMode` flag that hides server-dependent tabs, not a rewrite.
- **Tier 1 — self-hosted full stack:** docker-compose / one-command Bun setup on any
  $5 VPS; user brings their own X developer app + Premium. The README says plainly:
  "this tier costs ~$0.15/day and 30 minutes of setup; here's exactly what you get for
  it." Honesty *is* the differentiation — every competitor hides the costs.
- **Tier 2 (post-October, only if pulled):** hosted multi-tenant. Explicitly out of
  scope now, stays rejected.

**Chrome Web Store:** the v1 exclusion predates this pivot. An unpacked-extension
install kills Tier 0's five-minute promise. Submitting is ~$5 and a few hours of
manifest/privacy work. **Recommendation: reverse the exclusion and submit during Wave

**Electron: rejected, again, deliberately.** It serves installs, not impressions; it
duplicates the extension+server for weeks of work; and the product's soul is that it
*rides x.com* — a side panel next to the timeline. Revisit in 2027 if ever.
