What transfers: **one question, one screen, no argument in the post.** His referendums are under 46 characters. His receipts posts name a thing everyone touched this week and ask for a number. He never explains why the question matters — the reader supplies that, in the replies.

What does *not* transfer: his all-lowercase register (a borrowed signature reads borrowed at 51), his milestone posts, his link farms. Also his subjects are his — every post below has its own.

**The engagement bet, stated once.** Each post pegs to something dated in the last 7 days that a reader with an app, a SaaS or an agent bill has money or identity riding on, and asks a question that resolves to *yes/no*, *a number*, or *a pick from a list*. That is the cheapest reply type on the platform and it is what all four of his winning shapes have in common. The seed under every post then challenges the credibility of whichever side will be the majority — that is what restarts the thread among people who already replied.
