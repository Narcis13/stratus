## 3. How to fire them

**Order:** #1 and #10 are the two era reframes — the jackpot shape. Fire #1 first, at your single best slot of the week, and hold #10 for the following week. Never two of the same skeleton in one day. Rough sequence: **1 → 5 → 3 → 7 → 2 → 8 → 4 → 9 → 6 → 10**.

**Slot:** best-times is not usable yet. The Friday-08:00 cell shows avgViews 481,806 but that's n=3 containing the 1.44M outlier — a mean of one event, not a signal. The most trustworthy cell is **Tuesday ~14:00 local, n=5, avg 5,342**. Treat that as the anchor and re-check once the gate has real n.

**Per post:**
- 12+ minutes between anything. Your own monitor flags bursts, and so does X.
- Answer your own post within 60 seconds with the seed reply. It seeds the thread and it's the first brick of the own-thread block.
- No link in the post, ever. Aside from the algorithm, a URL in the text bills $0.20 vs $0.015 (CLAUDE.md invariant #1).
- No hashtags, no media on these. Your text-only median is 421 vs 201 with media.

**Spike protocol** — trigger is >5k views in the first hour, or >2k in 30 minutes:
1. Cancel the rest of the day's plan.
2. Reply substantively to **every** commenter for 48h. This alone can double the event.
3. Morning-after quote tweet with a data update.
4. Sequel question on day 3 (your 07-20 rerun did 19,038 — reruns work, they just don't repeat the jackpot).
5. Pin the winner for 72h.
6. Log it — it becomes next week's `data_comparison` post.

**Expected yield:** ~1 in 10 clears 20k, ~1 in 50 goes nuclear. Ten posts is roughly one mid-hit and a real lottery ticket. Don't judge any single one; judge the batch.
